Four tools, one standard

Simple enough for 30 brand owners to adopt, strict enough for an agency to be measured against.

Local SEO Standard

One checklist every brand meets: Google profile, consistent listings across top directories, service and city pages, schema, and call and form tracking by brand.

Brand Priority Score

Gap to standard × local search demand × average job value, from ServiceTitan and GA4. Fix first where visibility adds the most booked jobs.

Compliant review engine

A request after every completed job, replies within 48 hours. No incentives and no filtering of unhappy customers, as the FTC rule in force since October 21, 2024 requires.

Agency scorecard

Monthly, per brand: work shipped vs. plan, map-pack rank on top service and city searches, organic leads and booked jobs.
