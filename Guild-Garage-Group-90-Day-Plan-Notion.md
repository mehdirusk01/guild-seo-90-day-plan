# Guild Garage Group: 90-Day Local SEO Plan

> A 2-minute read by Mehdi El Asbat, applicant for the SEO Manager role. Built from public information only.

## The problem I think you're hiring for

Guild needs ~30 brands to win on Google, but each built its own site and profiles before joining, so you need one standard that turns visibility into booked jobs.

- **Search is your cheapest channel:** your posting says organic search, Google Business Profiles and brand websites produce Guild's lowest-cost booked jobs.
- **The portfolio keeps growing:** 32 partnerships since 2024, 8 of them in 2026 ([source](https://www.webull.ca/news-detail/15502295807697920)). Each one arrives with its own site, listings and habits.
- **The category is hostile:** Google removed 87.6% of the garage door listings examined in a 2022 [Sterling Sky study](https://www.sterlingsky.ca/google-maps-spam-study/), the highest rate of any industry studied.
- **Scaled rivals compete on proof:** A1 Garage Door Service claims 54+ locations and 140,000+ reviews ([a1garage.com](https://a1garage.com/)).

## My proposal: one standard, rolled out where it pays first

1. **Local SEO Standard:** one checklist every brand meets for Google profiles, directory listings and site basics, with call and form tracking by brand.
2. **Brand Priority Score:** gap to standard × local search demand × average job value, from ServiceTitan and GA4 data. Fix first where visibility adds the most booked jobs.
3. **Review engine:** a review request after every completed job through ServiceTitan, replies within 48 hours, no incentives and no filtering of unhappy customers ([FTC rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/08/22/2024-18519/trade-regulation-rule-on-the-use-of-consumer-reviews-and-testimonials), in force since Oct 21, 2024).
4. **Agency scorecard:** monthly, per brand: work shipped vs. plan, map-pack rank on top service and city searches, organic leads, booked jobs.

## My first 90 days

| Phase | Days | Deliverables |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Audit and baseline | 1–30 | Audit every brand (profiles, listings, sites, reviews) · Standard v1 approved by the CMO · Agency scorecard agreed |
| **2 · Fix the top 5 brands** | 31–60 | Priority Score ranks all brands · Standard applied to the top 5 · Review requests live in ServiceTitan |
| 3 · Scale and prove | 61–90 | Rollout plan for every brand · Checklist for every new acquisition · First scorecard to the CMO: before vs. after, by brand |

Phase 2 is where booked jobs should move first. Phase 3 makes the standard part of every new deal, so it keeps pace with acquisitions.

## Risks and assumptions

- **Public data only:** I don't know your agency setup, data access or brand-level numbers, so every target starts from a baseline we set together.
- **Owner autonomy:** partners keep unit-level ownership, so I'd co-write the standard with two brand leaders rather than impose it.
- **Profile edits carry risk:** Google has required [advanced verification](https://www.dasma.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/GoogleandFTCTakeAction_Fall2023.pdf) for garage door companies in key markets since 2017, so I'd edit in small batches with proof of address and licensing ready.

5+ years in web marketing on agency and advertiser sides (SEO, Google Ads, GA4, Google Business Profiles). I led the SEO audit of HommeDeMetier.be, a Belgian platform for tradespeople. Grew organic leads 35% in 6 months for a local services client.
